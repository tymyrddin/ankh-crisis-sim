# Research Exception

*"The truth may be out there, but lies are inside your head."* ~ Terry Pratchett, Hogfather

Notwithstanding the [Polyform Noncommercial Licence](LICENCE), the author explicitly permits use of this software for 
legitimate academic and educational research, free of charge.

## Who this is for

The Ankh-Morpork Crisis Simulator exists to make people think: about how cities fail, how institutions respond under pressure, how small decisions compound into large disasters, and how people in positions of responsibility behave when the drains are backing up and the river trolls are on strike.

If your work involves any of the following, you are welcome here without a commercial licence:

- Academic research into crisis management, urban resilience, or public administration
- Coursework, dissertations, or theses at any level of education
- Classroom use by educators incorporating the simulator into curricula
- Non-commercial conference presentations or demonstrations
- Game design research or serious games scholarship
- Policy research using simulation as a methodological tool
- Tabletop exercises run by non-commercial community or civic groups

## What we ask in return

We ask nothing legally binding. We ask, in the spirit of the Ankh-Morpork Patrician's approach to civic order — pragmatic, unsentimental, and quietly hopeful — that you:

- Attribute the simulator in any publication or presentation
- Share findings that might improve the software, if you are so inclined
- Do not pretend the simulation is more realistic than it is (it features a city with a river described as "almost entirely liquid")

## What this exception does not cover

This exception does not cover:

- Paid courses, workshops, or training programmes, even in academic institutions
- Consulting or advisory work delivered for a fee
- Integration into commercial products or platforms

If your research transitions into paid delivery, a [commercial licence](COMMERCIAL-LICENCE.md) is required.

## In the grey areas

Ankh-Morpork has always been a city that runs on pragmatism and good faith. If your use case seems to fall in a gap 
between "obviously fine" and "obviously commercial", [reach out](https://tymyrddin.dev/contact/), and we will work 
something out sensibly, without excessive paperwork or the involvement of the Guild of Lawyers.
